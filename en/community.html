Sanapolku community rules

Nicknames are visible to other players in shared results. Choose a name suitable for players of all ages.

Allowed nickname

A nickname must be 2–24 characters. It may contain A–Z and Å, Ä and Ö, numbers, spaces and _ . -. Letter case cannot reserve the same name twice.

Do not use your full real name, email address, phone number or other personal data.

Core rule

Play fairly and name kindly

A nickname must not insult, threaten, harass, deceive or reveal anyone’s personal data.

Prohibited content

  • hate speech, discrimination or degrading content;
  • threats, incitement to violence or harassment;
  • sexual, obscene or age-inappropriate content;
  • personal data, impersonation or misleading official names;
  • advertising, spam, scams or links to harmful services;
  • content encouraging manipulation of the game, service or results;
  • other unlawful or unsafe content.

Report an inappropriate nickname

Email laurelltm@gmail.com with subject “Sanapolku – nickname report”. Include the nickname, public UUID, round or time and a short reason when available. Never send the player secret or unnecessary personal data.

T&M Laurell evaluates reports as operator. Reports are not decided automatically and no handling time is promised.

In-app reporting and local blocking

In version 1.0, a public result row can open a report and locally block that UUID. A local block hides the player from public result rows on that device only; it does not delete server data or change other users’ views. Email reporting remains available.

Operator actions

The operator may request more information and remove or block a rule-breaking nickname or related use. Version 1.0 also includes server-side nickname filtering. Actions are assessed case by case.

For guardians

Help a child choose a nickname without a real name, age, school, location, contact detail or other identifying information. A guardian may report a nickname to laurelltm@gmail.com.

Accepting the rules

A player must accept and follow these rules when reserving a nickname and joining shared results.
