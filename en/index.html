How to play

1. Search

Start from any letter

Form Finnish words by moving between adjacent letters in the grid. Diagonal movement is also allowed.

2. Connect

Use each tile once

A tile may only be used once within one word. In the standard mode, words must have at least three letters.

3. Score

Longer words score more

Each distinct accepted word earns points. A three-letter word earns one point, and longer words earn progressively more.

Need help?

Competition and practice

Practice works locally without an online account or time limit. Competition players receive the same board and schedule. The daily challenge can be attempted repeatedly during the same UTC day, and the best result remains.

Public results

Competition results show the chosen nickname, public player ID, score and word count. Read the privacy notice and community rules for details.

Support in the app

Version 1.0 includes privacy, support and profile deletion under Settings. For ordinary support questions, you can also use the support address on this site.
