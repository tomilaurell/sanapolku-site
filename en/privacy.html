Sanapolku privacy notice

This notice explains what data Sanapolku processes, why it is used and what is visible to other players.

Updated 26 October 2026

Controller and contact

The publisher and data controller is T&M Laurell. Privacy questions and requests other than profile deletion can be sent to laurelltm@gmail.com.

Nickname registration

Competition play reserves a unique nickname chosen by the player. Registration does not request an email address, password or real name. The app sends a public UUID player ID, a player secret and the nickname to the service. The server stores only a one-way hash of the secret.

There is no email- or phone-based account recovery. If the secret stored in protected device storage is lost, the old nickname reservation cannot be restored automatically.

Data processed

Player ID
A random public UUID used for competition play. It does not itself contain a name, email address or device identifier.
Nickname
A unique nickname of 2–24 characters chosen by the player and shown in public round results. Do not use a real name or other personal data.
Player secret
A random secret stored in protected device storage. The server stores a one-way hash for verification. The secret is not public and must not be sent to support.
Game data
Competition mode, round and board identifiers, score, distinct word count, scoring version and an optional word-length distribution. Found words are not sent to the server.
Local data
Friends, blocked player IDs, achievements, settings, progress and up to 100 recent competition rounds. Practice history and detailed word data remain local.
Support messages and reports
If you contact support, T&M Laurell processes your email address, message and attachments to handle the request. Do not send a player secret or unnecessary personal data.

Purposes and legal bases

The nickname, player ID and competition data are processed to provide the service you request and perform the related agreement. Security, abuse prevention, technical maintenance and community-rule enforcement rely on T&M Laurell’s legitimate interest in keeping the service safe and operational. Support and privacy requests are processed to handle your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

What other players see

A published result shows the public UUID, nickname, score and word count, and may include a word-length distribution. It is available for no more than 24 hours after publication. The nickname remains as it was when the result was published.

Email addresses, the player secret, friends, achievements, local history and found words are not included in public results.

Service providers

Sanapolku uses Supabase to receive competition data and nickname reservations and Amazon Web Services CloudFront to deliver published round results. Data travels over HTTPS. Provider-side technical processing follows their applicable agreements and practices.

What Sanapolku does not use

There are no ads, tracking, analytics, in-app purchases or payment processing. Sanapolku does not use contacts, location or the device address book.

Retention and deletion

  • The player ID, nickname and secret hash remain until the profile is deleted.
  • Private score data remains for no more than the round’s 24-hour lifecycle or until profile deletion.
  • An immutable public result remains available for no more than 24 hours. A UUID and nickname published before deletion may remain until that period ends. A device that already downloaded the result may keep its local cached copy until the same expiry.
  • After deletion, only a UUID tombstone remains for 24 hours to prevent delayed requests from recreating the ID. It contains no nickname or secret hash.
  • Local history and result cache are each limited to 100 rounds and are also removed when app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

Technical service logs are retained only as needed for security, diagnostics and provider legal obligations. The exact duration depends on current Supabase and AWS settings; no single fixed log-retention period has been confirmed. You can ask the controller for current details.

Delete the profile and related service data in the app by following the deletion instructions. Deletion is not requested by email: the app securely verifies it with the secret stored on the device.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights of access, rectification, restriction, objection to legitimate-interest processing and portability of data you provided. For those requests and privacy questions, contact laurelltm@gmail.com. Profile deletion is performed in the app. You may also lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.

Never send the player secret. A request may require the public UUID and safe additional information to locate the correct nickname reservation.

Changes

This notice is updated when Sanapolku’s processing, providers or publication method changes. The latest version is published here with an update date.
